Final project instructions supplied by the project owner on 20 September 2026

The report is compulsory and is what is marked, the presentation is given in class.
One report per team. ONLY ONE SUBMISSION PER GROUP.
Clearly state all group members' names and student numbers on the report.
Page limit: 20 pages plus appendix. No page limit for appendix.
Submit a PDF or Word file on Blackboard, with team name and all members' names on page one.
The source says nine sections but explicitly enumerates the following eight, followed by the appendix.

1. The decision: Continue, pivot, narrow or stop. Put the real decision on page one.
2. Problem and customer: Who has this problem, and how do you know it is not just you?
3. The product: What it is, what a customer receives, the complete workflow, customer cost and your cost to make one.
4. Show the product: Pictures, screenshots, links; someone outside the team using it if suitable; first version next to current version.
5. What you tested: What you believed, did and observed, including failures.
6. Model and numbers: Where money comes from and whether the arithmetic works.
7. Commitment: Obtained commitments and proof. Failed commitments and rejections also count.
8. Limitations: What is still unknown.

Appendix: List every factual claim, what it rests on and where proof is. Evidence must be an action by a person with a date and count, a source that can be produced, or a recorded experiment. Rewrite or delete unsupported claims. Supporting screenshots and product information may be included.
Grading prioritises authentic evidence and judgement. A well-evidenced stop can score better than an unsupported continue decision.

Presentation: Last class, team or designated presenter, seven-minute hard limit, questions afterwards.
Cover these four sections in order: (1) where the venture is, for whom, and stage reached; (2) evidence and commitment, including market experience from problem through prototype to MVP; (3) working product demonstration; (4) decision and next 90 days.
Slides are optional or recommended. The report is marked. An attendance-exempt team must upload a video with the same four parts and seven-minute limit, everyone appearing and speaking, with the report by the same deadline.
No calendar deadline is included in the supplied text.
